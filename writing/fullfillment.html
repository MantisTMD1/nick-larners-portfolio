God damn I love food.It’s my absolute jam,Don’t care if its turkey-Or chicken-Or ham.

If samples are free,you’ll see meAt the stand.I’ll be lickin my chopsAnd straight rubbin my hands.

If I had my wayI wouldeat-eat-eat-eat.I would gobble down turkeys,And shovel in sweets.

And they say stolen candyIs never as sweet,But that’s never stopped meFrom taking a treat.

Gonna eat till I popAnd I might just keep going.Don’t care if i'm brokeAnd my belly is showing.

I’ll get nice and bigAnd I’ll just keep on growing.And if I lose my legs,Then I’ll just get to rolling.

Folks tell me I’m fatAnd I say“See you later.”I’m writing off loved ones.“Who needs em?They’re haters!”

My mother is worried,My father disowned me.But I can’t hear hate,Thru my fort of bologna.

Catch me at a buffetWith a plate of each thingA pancake wrapped pickle,French fried onion rings.

Should I say a prayer?For this bountiful feast?Eh-Why waste the time,I got good food to eat
