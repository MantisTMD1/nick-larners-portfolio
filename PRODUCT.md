# Larner Co

Portfolio of Nick Larner, a video editor and motion GFX artist (his own title, from his resume) in St. Paul, Minnesota.

- **Audience:** producers, agencies and brand clients deciding whether to hire or contact Nick, plus curious people who found his films.
- **Job of the site:** show the work (film, motion graphics, food reels, editorial credits) and make it easy to get in touch.
- **Voice:** first person, dry, self-deprecating and funny. Nick's own words are kept as written.
- **Brand commitments:** the Fido cut-paper dog logo, crumpled black paper, gold and teal accents. These stay.
- **Constraints:** pure HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No frameworks, build tools or libraries.
- **Assumption:** the audience above is inferred from the existing site and should be confirmed.
