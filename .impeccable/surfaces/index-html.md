# Landing page brief

Scope: larner-co.com landing page redesign. Persuade/Experience: visitor sees the work and gets in touch.
Audience: producers, agencies and brand clients; people who found Nick's films.
Constraints: pure HTML, CSS, JS. Nick's copy kept. Email only, no phone. User asked to modernize while keeping the same paper background.
Unresolved: real MP4 for the food reel; About, Writing, Resume and Contact pages still point to the existing site.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The work on crumpled paper, with nothing else competing. Modern restraint against one analog material; refuses collage decoration and a stacked centered column.
OWN-WORLD: The original crumpled black paper fixed behind the entire page, dusk-teal fade in the hero, one gold accent, off-white text; Jost at medium weights with tight tracking; pill actions with arrows; lightly rounded media.
STORY: Meet the dry voice, see clients, watch FLUSH, watch reels, scan the credits, then email or read the writing. "That's it." ends the page.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky slim nav; Fido logo left across seven columns; intro lead, paragraph, -NL and links right across five columns. Pointer lights the paper like a torch.
FORM: User-pinned by their words: "can we modernize it but keep the same paper background". That pins the paper and modernization; other choices were the builder's, with no alternatives dealt. Seed key: none, roll not run.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
